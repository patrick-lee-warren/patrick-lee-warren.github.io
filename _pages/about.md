I am a Professor at Clemson University and direct the Media Forensics Hub, a cross-disciplinary
initiative focused on understanding the context, origins, and impact of modern media. My work
blends research, public engagement, and applied collaboration to help communities strengthen
information integrity.

I welcome collaborations, invited talks, and student inquiries. For publications, see the
publications page or my Google Scholar profile.
