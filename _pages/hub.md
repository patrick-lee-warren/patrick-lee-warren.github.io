The Media Forensics Hub at Clemson University builds society's capacity to understand the
context, origins, and impact of modern media. As part of the Watt Family Innovation Center,
the Hub connects scientific expertise with practical application by linking faculty with
students, academics with practitioners, and researchers with the public.

Media forensics at the Hub spans multiple disciplines and approaches - from history and case
studies to AI and machine learning, and from lab experiments to field work. The Hub provides
physical and digital space for collaboration among students, faculty, staff, journalists,
policymakers, practitioners, organizers, and the public.

Visit the Hub: https://www.clemson.edu/centers-institutes/watt/hub/
